The devtools panel matches its design: while it's on, every form has a dashed outline with its name on a tag, and clicking the tag selects the form, so the pick button is gone. Inspecting stays on after a click, and hovering a label inspects the field it labels. A form with an `id` the adapter made up shows as `form 1`, `form 2` and so on. Menus open from their buttons, the clear button replaces the chevron in the form chip, the panel is as wide as the dock, scrollbars are thin and dark, and every icon button has a tooltip.
