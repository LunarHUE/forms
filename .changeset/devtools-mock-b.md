The devtools panel is closer to its design. A row's flags are icons, and hovering one shows what it means and its detail: the errors for invalid, baseline against value for dirty, the last write for edited. The field card shows only value, baseline and the field's writes with their causes. A row's pointer button scrolls the field's element into view. A filter box narrows the Fields tab by name, with a toggle for just the fields with errors. The bar shows how the last submission went, such as `submit failed`.
