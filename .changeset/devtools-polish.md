The devtools panel no longer shows the last submission in its bar. A row's flags keep fixed places, a click anywhere on a row selects its field, and long values keep their start and end in the cards instead of widening them. The on-page field card drops the form's name and stays open while the pointer crosses from a label to its input. Outlines sit under the page's dialogs and drawers, and a hidden form, such as one in an inactive tab, no longer leaves an outline in the corner of the window.
